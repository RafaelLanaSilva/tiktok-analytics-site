Focused by design

A small tool built around clear, objective data.

The application is being developed for personal use with specifically authorized TikTok accounts. It uses TikTok's official authorization flow and requests only the access needed to identify an account and read its public videos.

It does not post content, sell personal information, or use advertising profiles. Learn more about the planned processing and your choices in the Privacy Policy.

How it works

From authorization to insight

  1. 1AuthorizeThe account owner approves access through TikTok.
  2. 2CollectThe application requests permitted video and metric data.
  3. 3OrganizeInformation is structured for filters and comparisons.
