Privacy Policy

Effective September 19, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how TikTok Analytics, operated by Rafael Developer, processes information when an authorized user connects a TikTok account. The current development version is intended for the operator and specifically authorized Sandbox testers.

2. Information processed

Depending on the permissions approved by the user and the data made available by TikTok's official APIs, the application may process:

  • basic profile information, such as an open identifier, display name, and avatar;
  • public video information, such as video identifiers, descriptions, publication dates, and duration;
  • video performance metrics, such as views, likes, comments, and shares;
  • authorization information required to maintain the connection, such as access and refresh tokens;
  • technical records needed to diagnose errors and protect the application.

The application does not request a TikTok password. Authentication takes place through TikTok's own authorization flow.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to connect an authorized account, retrieve permitted data, organize video metrics, display comparisons, preserve relevant metric history, maintain security, and troubleshoot the application.

4. Sharing and sale

TikTok Analytics does not sell personal information. The application does not use connected-account information for advertising or unrelated profiling. Information may be processed by infrastructure providers strictly as needed to operate the application or when disclosure is required by law.

5. Storage and security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to restrict access to stored information. Authorization credentials are treated as confidential and are not published in this website or its source repository. No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Retention

Information is retained only for as long as needed for the purposes described above, to maintain the authorized connection, or to satisfy applicable obligations. Development and test data may be removed when it is no longer useful for testing.

7. User choices and deletion

A user may revoke the application's TikTok access using the controls offered by TikTok. A request to access or delete information associated with a connected account may also be submitted through the contact channel below. Identity or account authorization may need to be verified before a request is completed.

8. Children's privacy

The application is not directed to children and is not intended to knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the application, its infrastructure, or applicable requirements evolve. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the current version.

10. Contact

Privacy questions and requests can be initiated through the project's GitHub Issues page. Do not include access tokens, passwords, or private account data in a public issue; request a private follow-up channel instead.
